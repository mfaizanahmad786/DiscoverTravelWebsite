Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
  • What documents do I need for Hajj?

    You will need a valid passport (with at least 6 months validity), passport-sized photographs, vaccination certificates (including COVID-19 and meningitis), and completed visa application forms. We assist you with all documentation requirements.

  • How far in advance should I book?

    For Hajj, we recommend booking at least 6-8 months in advance due to limited quota allocations. Umrah can be booked 2-3 months ahead, though early booking often means better rates and hotel selections.

  • Are meals included in the package?

    Yes, all packages include daily breakfast. Deluxe and VIP packages also include lunch and dinner. All meals are halal-certified and prepared to high hygiene standards.

  • What is the age limit for Hajj?

    There is no specific age limit, but pilgrims should be physically and mentally capable of performing the rituals. Children under 18 must be accompanied by a guardian. Special arrangements can be made for elderly pilgrims.

  • Is travel insurance included?

    Basic travel insurance is included in all packages. We also offer comprehensive insurance options that cover medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost luggage for an additional fee.
